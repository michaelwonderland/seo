▲ Critical — read first

Organic search has collapsed roughly 84% since February

This is not a slow decline to be optimised away. In February the site ranked for 1,900 keywords and drew an estimated 5,552 organic visits a month. By July that was 278 keywords and 1,032 visits. Today it sits at 191 keywords and 892 visits. 815 keywords were lost outright.

The cause is visible in the data: the site was replatformed onto Shopify, and the old URLs were never redirected. Legacy .html product and brand pages return 404, not 301. On top of that, the new site exposes almost no indexable category pages — 98 of the top 100 indexed URLs are individual /products/ pages — so there is nothing left to rank on the terms that actually sell furniture.

Everything else in this report — the thin keyword set, the spammy link profile, the absence from every commercial SERP — sits downstream of those two facts. Fix them first; the rest is wasted effort until then.

02

What actually broke

Three findings, each verified directly against the live site and Google's index rather than inferred from the traffic curve.

Finding 1 — Legacy URLs return 404 instead of redirecting

The site moved from a legacy Magento-style structure (/american-leather-grisham.html, /shop-by-brand/a-c/american-leather.html) to Shopify (/products/carmet-sofa). Server IP 23.227.38.74 and a Cloudflare front confirm the Shopify stack. Old URLs were tested individually:

The only redirects in place are protocol and www normalisation — http:// hops to https://, and then the path itself 404s. There is no page-level redirect map. Every link, bookmark and ranking attached to the old catalogue is being thrown away.

Finding 2 — There is no category layer to rank with

Of the top 100 URLs Google has indexed for the domain, 98 are individual /products/ pages, one is the homepage and one is a /pages/ URL. Google reports roughly 1,850 indexed URLs — so the catalogue is being crawled, it simply has no collection or category pages carrying commercial intent.

This matters because that is precisely where competitors win. Modern Miami ranks #1 for "modern coffee table" (33,100 searches/mo) off a category page. MH2G ranks #1 for "contemporary white dresser" (6,600/mo). Wasser's has no equivalent page to compete with.

Finding 3 — Product URLs are 404ing on the new site too

The breakage is ongoing, not just historical. Of 1,000 linked pages sampled, 36 return 404 — and many are current-generation Shopify URLs (/products/sarasota-recliner-chair, /products/callum-recliner-chair, and the whole Comfort Relax recliner range). Products are being deleted or unpublished without redirects, which will keep bleeding rankings after the migration is fixed.

One caveat, stated plainly. Repeated requests to the site returned 429 Too Many Requests from Cloudflare. That is normal rate-limiting against a crawler hitting the same host repeatedly, and it is not evidence that Googlebot is blocked — but it is worth confirming in Search Console that Google's crawl rate isn't being throttled, since aggressive bot rules on a freshly migrated site would compound everything above.

Anchor text

762 of 1,449 backlinks carry an empty anchor (image or bare links) across 297 referring domains. Of the anchors that do carry text, almost all are the bare domain or brand name. There is essentially no commercial or category anchor text — nothing saying "luxury furniture Miami", "Italian furniture", or any category the business wants to own.

What's genuinely missing. For a 60-year-old Hallandale Beach showroom carrying Italian design brands, there are no links from local press, no design or interiors publications, no chamber or business association listings, and almost nothing from the manufacturer brands the store is an authorised dealer for. Those dealer-locator links alone are usually the easiest high-authority wins available to a retailer like this — and they are simply not there.

07

Where I'd start

A sequence rather than a list — each step depends on the one before it. This is the starting point for the plan, not the finished plan; the sizing and timeline are worth working through together.

STEP 01

Rebuild the redirect map

Export the legacy URL set from the old platform, from Google's index, and from the 392 referring domains. Map every legacy .html URL to its closest live equivalent and implement 301s in Shopify. Where no equivalent exists, redirect to the parent category rather than letting it 404.

Recovers the 815 lost keywords and reconnects 94 broken pages' worth of link equity. Nothing else works until this does.

STEP 02

Stop the ongoing 404 bleed

Put a rule in place so unpublishing a product redirects to its collection instead of returning 404, and clear the 36 currently-broken product URLs. Confirm in Search Console that Cloudflare's bot rules aren't throttling Googlebot.

The migration damage is still accumulating. This caps it.

STEP 03

Build the category layer that doesn't exist

Indexable, copy-bearing Shopify collection pages for the categories with real demand — sofas and sectionals, dining tables, coffee tables, bedroom, lighting, outdoor — and for the brands the store is an authorised dealer for. This is the single biggest structural gap and the thing every Tier 2 competitor is beating them with.

"modern coffee table" is 33,100 searches/mo and Modern Miami holds #1 with a category page.

STEP 04

Own the local commercial terms

Dedicated pages for Hallandale, Aventura, Sunny Isles, Fort Lauderdale and the Miami Design District, plus a properly built local presence. Wasser's already ranks 8–10 for the Hallandale terms — those are close-range wins that should be page-one top-three.

4,400/mo on "furniture stores miami" alone; peers hold these at difficulty scores of 0–6.

STEP 05

Clean the link profile, then build a real one

Disavow the blogspot network and the obvious spam domains. Then pursue the links that should already exist: manufacturer dealer-locator pages, South Florida design and interiors press, local business and chamber listings, and the trade site wasserstothetrade.com.

Camerich Miami out-ranks Wasser's locally on 53 referring domains. Quality is the gap, not volume.

STEP 06

Re-baseline and track

Lock in the current numbers as the floor, set up rank tracking against the Tier 2 set specifically, and measure recovery of the lost 815 separately from new growth — otherwise recovery will get reported as if it were gains.

Distinguishes "we fixed the migration" from "we grew the business".

Realistic framing. Steps 1–2 are recovery, not growth — they should return a meaningful share of the February baseline (roughly 5,500 visits/mo) within two to three months of Google recrawling. Steps 3–5 are the actual growth programme and where the Tier 2 competitors get taken on. Treating step 3 as the starting point without doing steps 1–2 first would mean building on a site that is still throwing away everything it earns.
