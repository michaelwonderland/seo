Organic search audit

Personio · DE / UK / US

Personio ranks like a magazine, not like a software vendor.

Across all three markets, 82–98% of Personio's estimated organic traffic comes from informational keywords — minimum wage, Gen Z, PTO meaning. On the keywords where someone is actually shopping for HR software, it is outranked by every serious competitor in the category, in both Germany and the UK.

02

Two different competitor sets

The domains Personio fights for informational traffic are not the ones it fights for customers.

Competitors were derived from live SERP overlap on two keyword sets per market: 50 category money keywords (HR software, HRIS, payroll software, Bewerbermanagement­software and their variants — average CPC $47 in Germany, $113 in the UK), and 50 informational keywords taken from Personio's own top-ranking content.

The two lists barely overlap, and that is the finding. On informational SERPs Personio's rivals are Wikipedia, government portals, trade unions and statistics bureaus. On money SERPs they are HR software vendors — and there Personio is barely present.

07

What this means

Four conclusions that follow directly from the data above.

  • The content engine works, and it is pointed away from the product. personio.de outranks every union, ministry and trade body in Germany on employment-law content, and personio.com is the only vendor competing with GOV.UK. That is a genuine, hard-to-replicate asset — it is just attached to queries made by employees, not buyers.
  • The money SERPs are winnable, not locked. rexx systems leads Germany on 14 keywords with a fraction of Personio's domain footprint, and BreatheHR holds an average position of 1.9 in the UK on seven. These are low-volume, high-CPC terms where a focused page beats a big domain. Personio already ranks 5th and 7th on the two biggest UK terms — the gap is coverage, not authority.
  • Germany is the bigger miss. It is Personio's home market and largest footprint, yet it has 7 buying keywords in its top 1,000 against Sage's 84, and an average position of 21.2 on the money set against rexx's 3.6. The UK gap is wider in relative terms; the German gap is worth more.
  • Fix the internal path before adding pages. Personio has 859 German and 854 UK top-10 rankings on informational content. The routing problem — turning a reader of mindestlohn 2026 into a software evaluation — is a cheaper first move than trying to outrank Sage on payroll software from a standing start.

Method & caveats

  • Source. DataForSEO Labs ranked_keywords, keyword_suggestions and serp_competitors, Google, pulled 14 September 2026. Locations: Germany/German, United Kingdom/English, United States/English.
  • Depth. Ranking profiles cover each domain's top 1,000 keywords by estimated traffic, not its full index. Shares and counts are of that slice — personio.de's full index is 45,963 keywords.
  • Intent labelling. DataForSEO's own intent classifier is unreliable on head terms — it marks hr system ($204 CPC) informational. Keywords here are tagged transactional if the classifier says commercial or transactional or the keyword carries a product/purchase modifier (software, system, HRIS, payroll, Vergleich, Alternative, Preis, best). Everything else informational.
  • Keyword sets. 50 money keywords per market, built from phrase suggestions on 9 category seeds and deduplicated. DataForSEO clusters synonym variants onto one SERP, so several rows in the head-to-head tables share a result page; blanks on those rows mean no distinct SERP exists, not a lost ranking.
  • Traffic figures. Estimated (ETV), not measured. Treat them as relative magnitudes between domains, not as analytics.
  • Competitor selection. Top 3 transactional competitors were chosen by SERP visibility among HR software vendors. Comparison portals and publishers that rank higher (OMR, Für-Gründer, hr-software-vergleich, Haufe, smallbusiness.co.uk) are listed in section 02 but excluded from the head-to-head as placement targets rather than rivals.
